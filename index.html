Our Living Heritage

Rooted in Tradition.
United by Sangathani.

Founded in 1956 by visionaries of South Kolkata, Sangathani Club began as a close-knit neighborhood gathering with a singular pledge: to keep the soul of Bengali culture beating brightly for generations to come.

What started as a modest bamboo mandap with handwritten invitations has blossomed into one of Bengal's most cherished socio-cultural institutions. Today, Sangathani is more than a Puja organizer — it is an open family where devotion meets grassroots social service, classical literature inspires modern youth, and every resident finds a home.

Mritshilpi: The Clay That Breathes Life

For our landmark 71st year, Sangathani Club pays homage to Bengal’s sacred soil and the timeless potters of Kumartuli. An evocative installation created entirely from sustainable river silt, straw armature, and eco-friendly vegetable pigments — reflecting how clay transcends mortality to embody divine motherhood.

Humanitarian Service

Celebration with a Purpose

At Sangathani Club, festive jubilation finds its highest meaning in selfless service. Our annual blood donation camp — held in partnership with the Central Blood Bank and local hospitals — is one of the most trusted drives in South Kolkata.

Beyond blood camps, our community outreach wing runs free cataract surgeries, annual winter blanket distributions to impoverished shelters, and educational scholarships for underprivileged local children.
